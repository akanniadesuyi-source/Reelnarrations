Clipper tools

More editing tools can be added after the core clip workflow is tested.

Scene detection

Finds visual changes in a video. It does not understand which moments are viral or most interesting.

Manual cutting

Choose exact start and end times for the parts you want.

Vertical resizing

Crop videos into 9:16, 16:9, or square format.

How to use

  1. Choose and upload your video.
  2. Detect scene changes or add clip times manually.
  3. Select the clips you want and choose the output format.
  4. Render, preview, and download your clips.

Keep this page open while clips are rendering. Large videos may take time on your phone or server.
